## A note on recruiter email IDs

Individual recruiters' direct emails are almost never published — they sit behind paid databases (ZoomInfo, RocketReach, Prospectoo) and scraping/guessing them isn't reliable or appropriate. What **is** verified and public is listed above (`careers@sarvam.ai`, `founders@drdroid.io`, `support@drdroid.io`, `founders@ravenapp.dev`). For everyone else, the highest-reply-rate channels in practice are:

1. **YC Work at a Startup profile** — founders message you first.
2. **Wellfound applications** — go straight to hiring managers.
3. **LinkedIn DM to founders/talent leads** at seed-stage startups (e.g., Rishika Singh at Sarvam; ContraVault, Raven, and Sujho founders) — at <50-person companies founders read these themselves.
4. **Cold email to verified addresses above** with a 3-line pitch + resume; lead with "co-founder who shipped two production platforms" — that's your differentiator over other 2026 grads.
